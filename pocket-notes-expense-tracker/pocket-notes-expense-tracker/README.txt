POCKET NOTES — EXPENSE TRACKER

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Keep index.html, style.css and script.js together in one folder.
3. Open index.html in Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox.
4. On a phone, open index.html in your browser. It is responsive.

FEATURES
- Add income and expenses
- Monthly balance, income and spending totals
- Category spending breakdown
- Last-seven-days chart
- Recent transactions and filters
- Saves entries in this browser using localStorage
- No account or server required

Your entries stay in the browser on the device where you add them. Clearing browser site data may erase them. This is a front-end tracker, not a bank-connected app.
